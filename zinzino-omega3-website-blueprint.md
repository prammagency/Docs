# Omega-3 Oil Website — Content + Scroll/3D Experience Blueprint

> Source of truth: Zinzino BalanceOil+ (omega-3 product line). This document
> distills only the information that matters for a minimalist, high-impact
> single-product site, then maps it to a scroll-driven 3D narrative on a black
> background.
>
> Research pulled from Zinzino's official explore/product/blog pages
> (June 2026). All numbers below are taken from their published specs.

## Part 1 — The Key Information (only what's essential)

The whole product story compresses into **one sentence**:

> **Fresh, fragile omega-3 from wild fish, kept alive and absorbable by
> olive polyphenols — so it actually reaches your cells.**

Everything else is supporting evidence. Below is the minimum set of facts
worth putting on the page.

### 1.1 The problem (the hook)
- The modern Western diet is **high in omega-6, low in omega-3**.
- **Over 6 billion people are insufficient** in omega-3.
- An out-of-balance omega-6:3 ratio is the thing the product fixes.

### 1.2 Why most omega-3 fails (the differentiator — lead with this)
- Omega-3 is **naturally fragile and starts to oxidize the moment it leaves
  the sea**. Oxidized (rancid) omega-3 is useless to the body.
- Standard refining removes contaminants **but also strips out the
  polyphenols** that naturally protect the oil.
- **Polyphenols bind to the omega-3**, act as antioxidants that prevent lipid
  oxidation, and **safely carry the omega-3 all the way into the cell
  membranes**.
- Net message: *Not all omega-3 is created equal. It comes down to freshness,
  type, and how much your body can actually absorb.*

### 1.3 What it is (the formula)
- **60% high-grade fish oil + 40% extra-virgin olive oil + naturally sourced
  vitamin D.** No artificial colors, flavors, or preservatives.
- **Fish oil:** 6,600 mg, unique **20/10 ratio of EPA to DHA**, from small,
  wild-caught **sardines, mackerel, anchovies** (Pacific & Atlantic).
- **Olive oil:** 4,000 mg early-harvested Southern European **Spanish Picual**
  olives, cold-pressed, **polyphenols above 750 mg/kg**.
- **Vitamin D3:** 20 µg, naturally sourced from sheep's wool (lanolin).
- EPA/DHA in their natural **triglyceride form** (better absorbed).
- Polyphenol blend: **hydroxytyrosol, tyrosol, oleuropein, oleocanthal,
  flavonoids.**

Per 12 ml serving (for a spec strip): **Omega-3 2,500 mg · EPA 1,300 mg ·
DHA 700 mg · DPA 150 mg.**

### 1.4 The proof (trust)
- **Scientifically proven, third-party tested, measurable effect in 120 days.**
- Goal: bring the **omega-6:3 ratio to 3:1 or below**.
- A **home blood test (BalanceTest)** measures your level before and after —
  this is the "proof", not a promise.
- **Friend of the Sea** certified; molecularly tested for toxins; sustainably
  sourced.

### 1.5 The benefits (keep to the authorized, concrete ones)
- **DHA** supports normal **brain function** and **vision**.
- **EPA + DHA** support normal **heart function**.
- **Olive polyphenols** protect **blood lipids from oxidative stress**.
- **Vitamin D** supports **immune system, muscles, and bones**.

> Minimalist rule: pick **4 benefit icons max**. Heart, Brain, Cells/Vision,
> Immunity. Don't list the regulatory fine print on the hero journey — park it
> in a footer/expandable.

## Part 2 — The Scroll Experience (scene direction)

**Visual system:** pure black background (#000) throughout, one continuous 3D
canvas behind sparse white/gold typography. Each "scene" is a pinned scroll
section; the camera and the hero object morph as the user scrolls (scroll =
scrubbed timeline, not autoplay). Think *one unbroken journey from tree → cell
→ bottle*, never a hard cut.

Your original flow is strong. I've kept its spine and tightened it into **6
beats**, added a scientifically-honest "why it's better" scene (the oxidation
shield), and made every object morph into the next so there are no jarring
transitions.

### Scene 0 — Hero: The Olive Tree
- **3D:** A single olive tree on black, gently lit, slow ambient sway. Faint
  particle dust/light catches. Camera holds wide.
- **Text (centered, fades in):**
  `Balance, by nature.`
  sub: *Fresh omega-3, protected by the olive. Proven in your blood in 120 days.*
- **Scroll cue:** a thin down-arrow / "scroll" hint.
- **On scroll:** camera pushes toward one branch, one olive grows in frame.

### Scene 1 — Zoom into the Olive → Polyphenols
- **3D:** Continuous dolly-zoom into a **single Picual olive** until it fills
  the screen, skin translucent. As we get close, glowing **gold particles**
  appear suspended inside the flesh — the polyphenols.
- **Interaction:** pinpoint/label callouts snap onto the glowing points as you
  scroll (hydroxytyrosol, oleuropein, oleocanthal…). Keep it to 2–3 labels,
  not all five — minimal.
- **Text beats (sequential, tied to scroll):**
  1. `This little fruit is a bodyguard.`
  2. `Cold-pressed Spanish Picual olives — polyphenols above 750 mg/kg.`
  3. *Powerful antioxidants that protect blood lipids from oxidative stress.*

### Scene 2 — The Squeeze: Fresh Juice / Why Polyphenols Matter
- **3D:** The olive **compresses and cold-presses**; a stream of golden-green
  oil flows down and the gold polyphenol particles pour out with it. The oil
  flows toward the right of frame, carrying the glow with it (this glow becomes
  the "shield" in Scene 4 — visual continuity).
- **Text:**
  1. `Pressed, never heated.`
  2. *Extra-virgin olive oil — Omega-9 plus the antioxidants that keep
     everything else alive.*
- **Purpose of this beat:** establishes polyphenols = protection, so the
  omega-3 payoff lands later.

### Scene 3 — The Herring / Wild Fish: Omega-3
- **Transition:** the stream of oil flows into deep water; camera follows it
  down and a **shoal of small fish (sardine/mackerel/anchovy — "herring"
  family)** swims into frame against black water with volumetric light shafts.
- **3D:** Fish swim along the scroll direction; one fish becomes the focal
  point. Camera tracks it.
- **Text beats:**
  1. `Omega-3 starts in the sea.`
  2. *Wild-caught sardines, mackerel & anchovies. 6,600 mg fish oil, a 20/10
     EPA:DHA ratio, in natural triglyceride form.*
  3. small spec strip fades in: `EPA 1300mg · DHA 700mg · DPA 150mg / 12ml`

### Scene 4 — Why This Omega-3 Is Better: The Oxidation Shield *(the money scene)*
> This is the scene you were unsure about. This is where the two ingredients
> finally connect — make it the emotional + scientific peak.

- **3D concept — split fate of a molecule:** Camera dives **into the fish**,
  down to a single **omega-3 molecule** suspended in dark fluid. Show **two**
  of them side by side:
  - **Unprotected molecule (left):** the instant it leaves the "sea" it starts
    to **dim, brown, crackle and break apart** (oxidize / go rancid). Cold,
    grey, dying light.
  - **Protected molecule (right):** the **gold polyphenols from Scene 2 flow
    in and wrap around it** like a glowing shield. It stays bright, intact,
    pulsing.
- **Payoff motion:** the protected, glowing omega-3 then **travels and docks
  into a cell membrane** (a luminous lipid bilayer) — literally showing
  "absorbed all the way into your cells."
- **Text beats:**
  1. `Omega-3 oxidizes the moment it leaves the sea.`
  2. `Most refining strips the polyphenols that protect it.`
  3. `We put them back. Polyphenols shield the omega-3 — all the way into
     your cells.`
- **Why it works:** it's true, it's unique to this product, and it visually
  justifies why olive + fish belong in the same bottle.

### Scene 5 — Convergence: Into the Bottle
- **3D:** Pull back from the cell. The **golden olive-polyphenol stream** and
  the **silver omega-3 / fish stream** spiral together and pour down into the
  **Zinzino BalanceOil+ bottle**, which assembles/fills on a black pedestal
  with a soft rim light. Liquid level rises with scroll; label resolves last.
- **Text:**
  1. `Fish oil + olive oil = results.`
  2. *60% wild fish oil · 40% extra-virgin olive oil · vitamin D3. Nothing
     artificial.*

### Scene 6 — Proof & Close (the only "flat" UI section)
- Background settles: bottle holds center, 3D motion calms.
- **The proof block (3:1):** big number animation — *"Get your omega-6:3 ratio
  to 3:1 or below — proven in your blood in 120 days."*
- **4 benefit icons:** Heart · Brain · Cells (vision) · Immunity.
- **Trust row (small):** Friend of the Sea · third-party tested · no artificial
  additives · home BalanceTest.
- **Single CTA:** `Test. Take. Re-test.` → primary button (e.g. *Start your
  balance* / *Get BalanceOil+*).
- Footer: regulatory health-claim fine print, ingredients, sourcing — collapsed
  by default.

## Part 3 — Recommendations & changes to your original idea

1. **Keep your spine — it's good.** Tree → olive → squeeze → fish → "better"
   → bottle is a clean narrative. I only resolved the one weak link.
2. **The "better omega-3" scene = the oxidation shield.** You weren't sure what
   the background should be here. Make it *inside the molecule*: unprotected
   omega-3 going rancid vs. polyphenol-shielded omega-3 surviving into a cell.
   This is the single most persuasive, on-brand idea on the whole site and it's
   scientifically accurate.
3. **Carry one visual thread the whole way:** the **gold polyphenol glow** born
   in the olive (Scene 1–2) is the same glow that shields the omega-3 (Scene 4)
   and one of the two streams that fills the bottle (Scene 5). It makes six
   scenes feel like one shot.
4. **Lead the messaging with freshness/absorption, not "omega-3 is healthy."**
   Everyone says omega-3 is healthy; almost no one explains the oxidation
   problem. That's your wedge.
5. **Anchor on two numbers, not ten:** **3:1** (the balance target) and
   **120 days** (proof). Let the detailed spec (EPA/DHA/mg) appear small, on
   the fish scene only.
6. **Substitute fish honestly:** the brand uses sardines/mackerel/anchovies,
   not literally herring. A herring reads great visually — fine to keep the
   silvery shoal aesthetically, but write copy as "wild-caught small fish."
7. **Performance/UX guardrails for the 3D:**
   - Scrub the timeline to scroll position (GSAP ScrollTrigger + a Three.js /
     R3F canvas, or pre-baked frame sequences if you want guaranteed smoothness).
   - Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: fall back to static stills + fades.
   - Mobile: reduce particle counts, keep the same 6 beats, lock to portrait
     framing of each hero object.
   - Keep text in real DOM over the canvas (accessibility + SEO), not baked in.
8. **Minimalism discipline:** one headline + one supporting line per beat. If a
   sentence isn't the hook, a number, or the CTA, it goes in the footer.
