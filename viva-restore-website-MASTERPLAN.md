## PART 1 — IDEATION (every key point to communicate)

### 1.1 What it is
- **Viva+** — an all-natural **nootropic** food supplement for mental wellbeing.
- Helps you **stay balanced and increase feelings of well-being naturally.**
- Positioned against the everyday enemy: **brain fog, fatigue, low mood, stress.**

### 1.2 The hero ingredient — affron® saffron
- Patented extract of **pure, premium saffron stigmas** (the three crimson threads
  of the *Crocus sativus* flower — the world's most precious spice).
- Standardised, **clinically proven** extract (**affron®** by Pharmactive).
- **Clinical proof:** randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial — **128
  healthy adults** with self-reported low mood took **28 mg/day** of affron® or
  placebo. After **4 weeks**, the affron® group **significantly improved in mood**,
  reporting reduced tension, depression and fatigue, decreased stress, and slightly
  improved sleep.
- **Mechanism (the story):** saffron's actives (crocin, safranal) **modulate the
  body's stress pathways**, while magnesium and B-vitamins **support the production
  of serotonin and dopamine** — the "feel-balanced" neurotransmitters.

### 1.3 The supporting cast (the full formula)
| Ingredient | Source | What it contributes (permitted/clinical claim) |
|---|---|---|
| **affron® saffron extract** | Premium saffron stigmas | Mood, mental alertness, concentration, memory, reasoning |
| **Magnesium** | From seawater | Normal energy-yielding metabolism; reduces tiredness & fatigue; normal psychological function & nervous system |
| **Vitamin C** | Acerola berries | Reduces tiredness & fatigue; normal psychological & cognitive function; nervous system |
| **Iodine** | Mineral | Supports normal cognitive function |
| **Vitamin B6** | — | Normal psychological function & nervous system |
| **Vitamin B12** | — | Reduces tiredness & fatigue; nervous system |

### 1.4 Product facts / usage
- **60 tablets**, net weight **21 g**.
- **Suggested use:** Children 4–11: 1–2 tablets/day · Adolescents 12–17: 2–4/day ·
  **Adults 18+: 2–6 tablets/day.**
- **Vegan** — carries **The Vegan Society** trademark (no animal ingredients, not
  animal-tested).
- Part of Zinzino's **Restore** range (mental wellbeing), companion to the
  **Balance** range (omega/BalanceOil+).

### 1.5 Trust / proof signals to surface
- Clinically studied dose (28 mg) — not a token sprinkle.
- Patented, standardised extract (affron®) — consistent actives every tablet.
- Vegan Society certified.
- Double-blind, placebo-controlled human trial.
- Premium single-origin saffron sourcing story.

### 1.6 Who it's for (the emotional target)
The person who feels **foggy, frayed, and flat** — running on empty, stressed, can't
focus — and wants a **natural, proven** way back to feeling like themselves. The site
sells the **return to calm clarity**, not the chemistry.

> ⚠️ **Regulatory note:** mood/stress claims rest on the affron® clinical data;
> vitamin/mineral claims (fatigue, psychological function, nervous system, cognition)
> are EFSA-permitted health claims. Before publishing in any market, confirm the exact
> approved claim wording and any required disclaimers for that region. Put all fine
> print in the footer (see Part 7).

## PART 2 — THE NARRATIVE (one camera flight, one recurring motif)

**The recurring motif = "the calm light."** A warm saffron glow is *born* in the
flower's red threads, *released* as the extract, *travels up into the mind*,
*dissolves the grey stress-fog*, and finally *fills the bottle*. It is the exact
structural twin of the gold-polyphenol thread in the omega film — one motif carried
unbroken through all six beats.

**One continuous camera. No cuts.** Scroll = scrub the flight forward; stop = freeze;
scroll up = rewind.

```
 Crocus flower ─▶ saffron threads ─▶ glow released ─▶ up into the mind
       ─▶ fog dissolves into calm (CLIMAX) ─▶ streams fill the bottle ─▶ hero
```

| Beat | Scene | Story job |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Hero — the flower in the dark** | Calm, premium, "by nature" |
| 1 | **Into the threads** | The rarest spice; the active heart (affron®) |
| 2 | **The glow released** | The clinically-dosed extract awakens; minerals join |
| 3 | **Into the mind** | The glow rises into a luminous neural network |
| 4 | **The calm** *(climax)* | Grey stress-fog dissolves; serotonin sparks; balance |
| 5 | **Into the bottle + Proof/Close** | Convergence → hero bottle → numbers → one CTA |

## PART 3 — VISUAL SYSTEM / DESIGN SPEC

Same premium house style as BalanceOil+ (so the agency system stays coherent), but
re-keyed from **olive-gold/green** to **saffron-amber/crimson + a calm tone**.

### 3.1 Colour
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#000000` | Pure black, everywhere |
| **Saffron (primary accent)** | `#E5A23C` | The warm glow, key headlines, CTA |
| **Crimson thread (deep accent)** | `#C85A33` | The saffron stigmas, climax warmth |
| Text primary | `#FFFFFF` | Headlines, body |
| Text secondary | `rgba(255,255,255,0.55)` | Supporting lines |
| **Calm (clarity tone)** | `#9AA6D6` | The "clear mind" — used only in Scenes 3–4 |
| Fog grey | `rgba(150,150,160,0.45)` | The stress-fog being dissolved (Scene 4) |

> The restraint is the design: black + saffron + one calm tone. No other colours.

### 3.2 Typography (unchanged from the system)
- **Headlines:** Cormorant Garamond — Regular & SemiBold
- **Body:** DM Sans — Light & Medium
- **Spec/mono:** DM Mono — Regular
- Import all from Google Fonts.

**Type scale**
| Role | Spec |
|---|---|
| Hero headline | 72px Cormorant Regular |
| Scene headline | 56px Cormorant Regular |
| Supporting line | 18px DM Sans Light |
| Spec strip | 13px DM Mono, letter-spacing 0.12em, uppercase |
| Proof number | 120px Cormorant SemiBold |
| CTA button | 16px DM Sans Medium, letter-spacing 0.04em |

### 3.3 Motion
- **Scroll scrub:** GSAP ScrollTrigger, `scrub: 1.5`, `ease: "none"`.
- **Text enter:** opacity 0→1 + translateY 24px→0, **0.7s**, `power2.out`.
- **Text exit:** opacity 1→0 + translateY 0→-12px, **0.4s**, `power2.in`.
- Text fully visible between **15% and 80%** of its scene's scroll window.
- **Never scroll-jack.** Native scroll drives the scrub.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: static stacked sections, simple fades, no scrubbing.

## PART 8 — PRODUCTION RUNBOOK

### 8.1 Tooling (locked — same as omega)
- **Stills — Replicate:** FLUX.2 (primary), Imagen 4 (flower + mind realism shots).
- **Video — Replicate:** `kwaivgi/kling-v3-omni-video` with `start_image` + `end_image`.
  Params: `start_image`, `end_image`, `prompt`, `duration: 5`, `aspect_ratio: "16:9"`.
  Backup: Veo 3.1.
- **No Higgsfield** — it's a UI over the same models; Replicate only.
- **Front-end:** HTML/CSS/JS + GSAP ScrollTrigger.

### 8.2 Settings cheat-sheet
| Thing | Setting |
|---|---|
| Stills | FLUX.2 (Imagen 4 for flower + mind), fixed seed, black bg, 16:9 1920×1080 |
| Clips | Kling latest Pro, start_image + end_image, 5s, 1080p, silent tier |
| Backup video | Veo 3.1 (only if a Kling clip is weak) |
| Final film | ~35s, 24–30 fps |
| Website motion | GSAP ScrollTrigger scrub 1.5; image-sequence on canvas for mobile |

### 8.3 Build order
1. Lock the 8 stills (fixed seed/style) — judge as a sequence, re-make weak ones first.
2. Confirm each end frame == next start frame.
3. Generate the 7 clips (Kling Pro); extra takes on Clip 5.
4. `ffmpeg` concat → review the seamless master; iterate clips, not stills.
5. Composite the **real Viva+ bottle photo** into Scenes 5–6 (AI can't draw the label).
6. Build the site: GSAP scrub + fixed canvas + HTML text overlays per scroll window.
7. Add mobile (image-sequence canvas) + `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback. Ship.

### 8.4 Browser implementation note
One scroll-progress number (0→1) drives **both** the video playhead **and** the text
overlay windows — they can't drift. Desktop: `<video>` `currentTime` scrub (encode with
frequent keyframes, `ffmpeg -g 1`). Mobile/iOS: numbered frame sequence drawn to
`<canvas>` (smoother than video seeking on Safari). Headlines stay real DOM text over
the canvas (SEO + accessibility), never baked into the video.
